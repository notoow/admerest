## Browser checks

Used the desktop app browser at 1440×1000, 412×915, 360×800 and landscape 915×412. These are viewport checks, not tests on physical phones.

- Playground: invalid input, Apply from zero, completion, input 3100 + 100 → 3200, size switching, box view, detail view, tower registration and Eiffel comparison (3200 × 6 cm = 192 m).
- Exploration: mobile-layout joystick and look-pad drags change position/yaw; release stops movement. Landscape controls fit. Browser Back exits. Desktop camera position and target match before/after exploration. Focus remains within the exploration controls.
- Ranking: verified + Japan produces the empty state; reset restores three records and ad placement. Inquiry Back closes without leaving ranking. Direct ranking entry settles below the header.
- Record wizard: empty-field validation, sample record, eight size/type bins, 1250 cases independent of 1005 sheets/75 m, preview reveal, comparison return, local save/reload and removal of the test draft. Email draft retains entered aggregates. No email was sent.
- ADM notes: four-size overview, phone/card selection, actual-size popup, calibration adjustment/save/reset, close/Back and quiz interaction. At 360×800 the calibration card and adjustment controls are fully visible together.
- Videos: portrait Short and landscape wide video playback; end-of-Short replay/next screen; Back removes the iframe and returns to the article.
- Game: start, place, miss/game-over, pause and resume. The existing personal-best display remains intact.
- No page-level horizontal overflow on checked views; no application console errors in the final main-page run.

## Automated checks and limits

`npm run check`: 60 tests passed, including new history-dismissal races, editable-input quick additions and email-field transfer checks; existing asset, geometry, flight, video and local service tests passed.

Real Samsung/Android Chrome touch arbitration, simultaneous two-thumb gestures, browser chrome changes and sustained mobile GPU performance still require a physical-device pass. Desktop pointer drags on a mobile viewport do not establish that coverage. OS email-client opening/sending was intentionally not exercised. No backend or external account was created.
